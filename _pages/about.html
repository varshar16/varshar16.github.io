About Me

I am a PhD student at the University of Chicago, advised by Professor Andrew A.Chien. I am interested in designing sustainable computing systems. My research focuses on developing carbon accounting methodologies for research computing systems, sustainable storage systems, and carbon information services.

Research Areas:

Sustainable Computing, Cloud Computing, Distributed Storage Systems.
