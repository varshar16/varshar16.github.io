About Me

I am a PhD student at the University of Chicago, advised by Professor Andrew A.Chien. I study the design of environmentally sustainable large-scale computing systems. My research explores how to perform carbon assessment of computing systems and validate the assessment model with limited data and minimal effort, as well as how to reduce the carbon footprint of computing systems.

Research Areas:

Sustainable Computing, Cloud Computing, Distributed Storage Systems.
